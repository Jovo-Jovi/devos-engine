- **What the copies change,** for the report:
  - **`payload/contract/CONTRACT.md`, version `1` (`lib/contract.mjs`).** Before it is accepted, it says:
    - what the engine is, and that it works fully on its own;
    - that the DEV_OS Control Tower is a private service operated by Jovo-Jovi, not part of the engine and not available to its users;
    - that linking is optional and asked right after acceptance, with no answer chosen;
    - exactly what a link request and each event carry, and what is never sent;
    - the address and the protocol: HTTPS to `https://devos-control-tower.vercel.app`;
    - the permission: `id-token: write`, given only to the `report` job, which runs no AI agent;
    - how to turn it on or off: `DEVOS_REPORTING`.
  - **`lib/init.mjs`:**
    - right after `accept`, the question `LINK_QUESTION`, answered `yes` or `no`. Any other answer is asked again, and after three, setup stops with nothing written;
    - the answer is kept as `registry_link` in `devos/answers.json`;
    - the summary gains `Control Tower link: …`.
  - **`lib/plan.mjs`:** `buildAnswers` takes the answer, and a page plan without `link: true` or `link: false` is refused.
  - **`lib/setup.mjs`:** a yes sets the repository variable `DEVOS_REPORTING` to `on` and names it among the variables; a no sets nothing.
  - **`lib/ui/app.js`:** the contract step adds the same question. Neither answer is selected, and "Accept and continue" stays disabled until the contract is accepted and an answer is chosen. The plan carries `link`.
    - Remembered choices never carry the answer to the next project (`rememberChoices` keeps no `link`, unchanged).
  - **`devos-cycle.yml`:** the `report` job runs only when `vars.DEVOS_REPORTING == 'on'`.
  - **`engine.defaults.json`:** `registry.url` is `https://devos-control-tower.vercel.app`; its line is in `tools/specifics-allow.json`.
  - **The rest:**
    - `AUTOMATION.md`: `DEVOS_REPORTING` is off when not set, and on only after a yes;
    - PR-62 in `PRECEDENTS.md`;
    - `README.md`: a section "The DEV_OS Control Tower (optional)", and "Your keys and data";
    - version `0.3.0`;
    - `tools/sources.mjs` labels.

    - `test/consent.test.mjs` (new), (a)–(f), one per requirement: disclosure before acceptance, the terminal question, no answer means no setup, only a yes sets the variable, the page plan and remembered choices, the job condition;

    - `link: false` in the page plans (`plan`, `ui-server`);
    - the contract version `1` (`init`), the job condition (`report`), the version (`install`, `upgrade`, `version`).
