### For the owner, after the merge

- **Projects pick it up with `devos upgrade`** (1.12.0, a pull request). Only a full-scope plan has a launch task.
  An existing project gets one when its reviewer writes the last task's fence with a `- Launch:` line, or when you
  ask for it with a change request.
- **The reviewer's readings, yours to change:**
  - **Speed is a lab measure** on the local production build, without network or CPU throttling. It catches a heavy
    page and late layout shifts. Field data from real devices is for after launch.
  - **The headers are a baseline that every ASVS level-1 site can meet.** HSTS's preload and `includeSubDomains` are
    not required, and a CSP is required but not judged strict: the reviewer judges it under V3.
  - **Any axe violation fails,** as `a11y` does for UI tasks, not only serious or critical ones.
  - **The audit is the full `pnpm audit`,** dev dependencies too, as at E59. A `pnpm.auditConfig` waiver with your
    recorded decision still applies.
  - **Waivers are lines in `docs/DECISIONS.md`,** which you or an intake pull request write, never an agent of the
    cycle.
  - **The ASVS chapters a subject adds** (sign-in, sessions, tokens, OAuth, uploads, cryptography) are read on deep
    tasks only. A risk-1 or risk-2 task does not pay for them.
  - **V17 (WebRTC), the appendices, and sharp edges' other languages and case studies were left out.** No DEV_OS
    stack needs them, and `language-specific.md` covers each language in short.
