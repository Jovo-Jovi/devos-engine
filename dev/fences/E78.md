### Why these choices

- **On the adopt branch, not on `main`.** Adopt already builds everything in a worktree on `devos/adopt`. Moving the
  files there keeps your checkout and `main` untouched, and the pull request shows each move as a rename you can
  read or revert.
- **Moved, not renamed in place.** A Cursor rule left active could tell the builder to commit or push, which breaks
  "the runner owns git". A document left in `docs/` under another name would sit beside DEV_OS's and contradict it.
  In `docs/before-adopt/` nothing loads them, and the documents round carries over what still holds.
- **`.claude/settings.json` too goes to `docs/before-adopt/`** (owner attention): earlier, adopt's hint suggested
  `.claude/settings.local.json`. A local settings file is read beside DEV_OS's and can loosen its permission mode, so
  devos never makes one by itself. The pull request says how to bring back what you need.
