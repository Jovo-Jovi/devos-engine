### Why these choices

- **A copy on the branch, then the original out of the checkout.** devos never writes to your branch. The copy is
  committed on `devos/adopt` before the original moves, so the content is always in one of the two places. Moving it
  into `docs/before-adopt/` inside the checkout would not work: an untracked file at a path the merge adds makes
  `git pull` refuse.
- **Owner attention.** After an adoption with such files, they are no longer in your project folder: their content is
  in `docs/before-adopt/` once you merge, and the originals are in `~/.devos/adopt/<owner>__<repo>/before-adopt/`.
  Your `.gitignore` still lists them; the adoption's "committed although your .gitignore leaves them out" line names
  DEV_OS's files it forced.
- **Out of scope:** a file git tracks nowhere but that is not ignored (the survey already stops on a dirty checkout).
