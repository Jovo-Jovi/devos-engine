### Why these choices

- **Check before enabling.** PR-90's rule is that the review runs no command. A tool's documentation is a claim, and
  Codex reads files through its shell. Each tool is enabled only from its own line, recorded on your account (D7).
- **A red main does not hold the next task.** An adopted project's checks stay red until its first task adds what they
  need, so holding T01 kept learn-coptic red for good.
- **Close the later issue, not merge them.** The oldest keeps the history and the counts per cause (PR-70). Only issues
  carrying the supervisor's own label are touched.
