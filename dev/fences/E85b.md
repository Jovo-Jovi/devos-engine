### The change

1. **The hand-over takes every review file and fence up to 1 MiB.**
   - `devos-review.yml` *Hand the review to publish* copies the files of `docs/reviews/` and `docs/fences/` with
     `find … -type f -size -1048577c`, counted in bytes. GNU find rounds `-size -1M` up to whole MiB, so it matched
     only empty files.
   - The step then lists every file it hands over (`./docs/reviews/T01-a1.md`, …).
   - Its two optional copies use `if … fi` instead of `[ … ] &&`.
2. **The two jobs compare the verified tree, never a commit.**
   - `publish-verify.mjs verify` records `head_tree`, the tree of the head it verified, in `verify.json` and in its
     outputs. `head` moves on with publish's own commits, such as the usage records; `head_tree` stays.
   - The review job writes that tree to `.devos/reviewed-tree.txt`, in place of `review-head.txt`, and prints
     `review: <tool>, <mode> review of the tree <tree> (head <sha>)`.
   - Publish's *Reviewer (the review job's run, in the agent output contract)* compares `reviewed-tree.txt` with its
     own `head_tree`:
     - when they are equal, it prints `the review job reviewed the verified tree <tree>`;
     - when they differ or either is missing, it stops with `the review job ended success, but it reviewed the tree
       <a>, not the verified tree <b>; the review runs again (review only)`, which the supervisor already matches.
3. **A long review has room, and shows it is running.**
   - The reviewer step's limit is 120 minutes (was 75) and the job's 135 (was 90). `supervisor.mjs`'s fallback for
     `review` and `review / review` is 135.
   - The step prints `the reviewer is reviewing <task>: <n> min, review file <written|not yet>` every 5 minutes.
4. **`.gitleaks.toml`** allows the review job's own key names, `KEY_1` to `LALT_2` (with or without `secrets.`). They
   are one more alternative in the one secret-name pattern: `…|L?(KEY|ALT)_[0-9])$`. The release's history scan
   flagged `secrets.LKEY_2` in `two-jobs.test.mjs` and in E85's fence copy, a name and never a value.
   `own-images.test.mjs` (g) checks the new names, and that `LKEY`, `KEY_12` and `XKEY_1` stay refused.
5. **PR-102** in `PRECEDENTS.md` (live maximum PR-102); `AUTOMATION.md` §2 and the `devos-review.yml` row say "the
   reviewed tree"; `CHANGELOG.md` 1.13.17; the version 1.13.17 in `package.json`, `README.md`, the manifest and the
   six tests that name it. With the two tests above, 19 files in all.

Nothing changes for a project's configuration, and `devos upgrade` asks no question.
