### Why

- The review job ran the review tree's own `render-prompt.mjs` and `plan-agent.sh`.
- In that tree a method fix's files are the branch's: its reviewer prompt template, its `devos.config.json` (the
  reviewer's model), its runner scripts, its role kits and skills, and `.cursor/rules`.
- So a method fix was reviewed under the rules it changed. It still waits for your merge, but its review was not
  main's.
- A product task cannot change those files (verify rejects them, and `resolve` merges main into an open unit), so
  its review is unchanged by this task.

### The change

1. **`publish-verify.mjs review-tree` lays main's method files over the review tree**, as `verify` already does over
   publish's workspace. It uses the same list (`OVERLAY`): `.github`, `scripts/devos`, `scripts/guards`, `devos`,
   `devos.config.json`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude`, `.cursor`.
   - So in the review job, these are main's:
     - the prompt, rendered by main's script from main's template, config and skills;
     - the reviewer's tool, model, effort and next models (main's config);
     - `plan-agent.sh` and every script it runs, the tools' files and the role kits;
     - the files the reviewer's CLI reads as its own rules.
   - The reviewer step's commands do not change: the scripts they name in the tree are now main's.
2. **A method fix keeps its own method files in `.devos/branch-method/`**, at the same paths, for the reviewer to
   read and run. `.devos/diff.patch` still holds the whole change.
   - The folder is written only when the branch changes the method (`method_changed`).
   - The work job's evidence cannot stand for it: like `diff.patch`, any `branch-method` in the evidence is removed
     before it is written.
3. **Main's reviewer prompt says so:** the tree's method files are main's, and a method fix's own are in
   `.devos/branch-method/`.
4. **The manual:**
   - `AUTOMATION.md`: the `devos-review.yml` and `publish-verify.mjs` rows;
   - `PRECEDENTS.md`: PR-108, live maximum PR-108;
   - `CHANGELOG.md` under 1.14.0-rc.1;
   - `devos-review.yml`: its comments only.
5. **Tests:** `test/review-rules.test.mjs` (new):
   - (a) a method fix that changes its reviewer's template, the reviewer's model, `plan-agent.sh`, the Cursor
     reviewer kit and `.cursor/rules`: every method file of its review tree is main's, byte for byte; the branch's are
     in `.devos/branch-method/`, never the evidence's; and the reviewer step's own first line, run in the tree,
     renders main's prompt, with main's reviewer model;
   - (b) a product task's review tree holds main's method files and no `.devos/branch-method/`, whatever the evidence
     holds.

   Both fail on `next` @ 1b3a373.

8 files in all (7 changed, 1 new). For a project: nothing to set; `devos upgrade` updates the five payload files
(the script, the prompt, the workflow and the two method documents) and asks no question.
